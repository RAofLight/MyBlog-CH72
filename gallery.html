Courthouse in Noto

Walking along the main street in Noto, I came upon this beautiful courthouse. It's a popular tourist spot, where people like to take a lot of photos in front it...I waited over 10 minutes to get a good shot without any people blocking the view. The arcitecture in Noto is breath-taking...having people in the photo with the building would ruin the shot.

Streets of Noto

While my friends and I were exlporing the streets of Noto, we stumbled upon this little, tucked away street; leading to a decorative staircase. I felt the need to capture it on camera, so I took photo. The artwork on the staircase reminded me of the agave plants in Mexico.

Famous Steps of Noto

These steps leading up to a wood door are famous in Noto. They were made famous because they were in a scene from the film Malena, a very popular film that put Noto on the map. I did have images of me and my friends on these steps and infront of the door, but the images aren"t supported by the browser, so I couldn't upload them...we did have quite the photo shoot, and we attracted a crowd. It was so much fun!
